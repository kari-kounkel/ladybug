A note from Kari

Why this matters to me

As a 2022 graduate of Minnesota Adult & Teen Challenge, I know how hopeless and lonely life in addiction can be—and how difficult it is to make the changes needed to live a whole, healthy, God-facing life.

Ladybug, Ladybug is encouragement for the ladies to spend time getting to know who they were created to be and to grow the courage to live in that freedom once they graduate the program.

When you sponsor a copy, you help put that encouragement into the hands of a woman taking those steps herself.

With love, Kari
